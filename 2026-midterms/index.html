What the 120th Congress can pass

The new Congress convenes January 3, 2027. A majority can set the agenda, but it cannot override a veto, which takes two-thirds of both chambers. That makes 2027 a test of which reforms already have support across party lines. Several stand out, and the NSRA has stand-alone statutory text for each.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who won the 2026 midterm elections?

Democrats won majorities in both the House and the Senate on November 3, 2026, according to AP-based network projections. Democrats flipped Senate seats in Iowa, Kansas, Maine, North Carolina, Ohio, South Carolina, and Texas. Some House races and a few Senate and governor races remained uncalled as of the date shown on this page.

When does the new Congress start?

The 120th Congress convenes on January 3, 2027. Members elected in November 2026 are sworn in then, and new committee chairs and party leaders take over.

Can the new Congress override a presidential veto?

Not on party lines. Overriding a veto takes two-thirds of both chambers, which neither party holds. Bills with support across party lines, or that the President has already backed, have the clearest path to becoming law in 2027 and 2028.

Which cost-of-living bills could pass in 2027?

The strongest candidates are ones that have already drawn votes or endorsements from both parties: a congressional stock trading ban, a cap on institutional ownership of single-family homes, and prescription drug price reforms. The NSRA contains statutory text for each, and each title can be introduced as a stand-alone bill.
