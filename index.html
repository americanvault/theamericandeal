Balance the budget

A balanced budget, written into law

Under current law, CBO projects a deficit every year for the next decade, rising from $1.9 trillion to $3.1 trillion. The NSRA pays for every program it creates, cuts the deficit by collecting taxes already owed, closing special-interest loopholes and stopping waste, and then makes balance a legal requirement, not a hope.

Year 4
Paid for

Primary balance: everything the government spends, except interest on past debt, is covered by revenue.

Year 6
<3%

The deficit falls below 3% of GDP, a widely used benchmark for keeping the debt from growing faster than the economy.

Year 10
Balanced

Zero deficit, every year after. The bill’s revenue does most of the work; if it falls short, an automatic trigger closes the rest.

Deficit reduction
$5.9T

Over ten years from the bill’s own revenue, about $554B a year at full ramp.

Who is protected
Exempt

Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid, veterans, SNAP and the $0-premium plan are exempt from any automatic cut, and no tax change touches households under $400,000.

Even under conservative scoring
$355B

Year-one revenue with every behavioral and enforcement gain removed, still 2× the bill’s core obligations (Schedule G).

The targets and enforcement are in Sec. 3406(f). Dollar figures are projections from the NSRA’s own model on CBO’s February 2026 baseline; CBO has not scored the bill. Enforcement is suspended in a recession or war and resumes afterward.

How it passes

Five acts. Each one pays its own way.

The American Deal is 40 titles organized into five coordinated acts. Each act is self-funding and can pass on its own, so if some stall, Americans still win. Together they write a balanced budget into law and put about $9,000 a year back in a typical family’s pocket.

Act I

The American Fiscal Integrity Act

Closes the offshore tax gap, collects taxes already owed, stops Pentagon waste, and writes the balanced-budget path into law. It raises the money that pays for the other four.

Ways & Means · Finance · Armed Services
Act II

The American Health Security Act

$0-premium, $0-deductible baseline coverage through private insurers, G7 drug prices, $35 insulin, and dental, vision and hearing in Medicare.

Energy & Commerce · Finance · Veterans’ Affairs
Act III

The American Economic Security Act

Homes for families instead of funds, zero-interest student loans, $1,000 at birth for every child, paid leave, and science back in front.

Banking · Judiciary · Education · HELP
Act IV

The American Supply Chain Security Act

Grow, build and power America here: food security, cheaper energy, open broadband, and critical supply chains brought home.

Agriculture · Energy & Commerce · Transportation
Act V

The American Accountability and Data Rights Act

An independent watchdog against fraud and market rigging, real rights over your personal data, and government that works online.

Judiciary · Commerce · EPW
