The plan

What’s in the bill

Twelve plain-English promises, each written as ready-to-file legislation with its own one-page summary. Any member can introduce one tomorrow.

Title XXIX

Ban stock trading in Congress

Members, spouses, senior officials and judges divest within 180 days. Penalties cost more than any trade could earn.

Read more →
Title XIII

Pay what other countries pay for drugs

Prices tied to the G7 median, $35 insulin, and a cost floor so no drug is sold at a loss.

Read more →
Title XXI

Coverage with no premium

A baseline health plan at $0 premium through competing private carriers.

Read more →
Title XIV

Homes for families, not funds

A 50-home-per-metro cap on big investors, plus rewards for towns that allow more homes.

Read more →
Title XXXVIII

Paid leave and child care

12 weeks of paid leave that pays for itself, more child care slots, and an apprenticeship credit.

Read more →
Title XV

Zero-interest student loans

Interest on federal student loans drops to zero, existing and future.

Read more →
Title XXXV

Stop government waste

Measure and stop improper payments, end pocket rescissions, protect Inspectors General.

Read more →
Title XVII

Build faster

Permits on a fixed clock, published costs, and clean power from dams we already have.

Read more →
Title XXXVII

Lead the world in science

Federal research back to 1% of GDP, and green cards for U.S.-trained scientists.

Read more →
Title XXXIX

Government that works online

Retire decades-old systems on a deadline and build one secure front door.

Read more →
Title V

A start for every child

$1,000 at birth in the child’s Trump Account, plus up to $500 a year matched for working families.
