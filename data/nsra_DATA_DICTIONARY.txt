NSRA MACHINE-READABLE DATASETS - DATA DICTIONARY
Methodology version: v2026.1    Last updated: 2026-07-11
Publisher: TheAmericanDeal.org (National Sovereignty and Resilience Act)
Status: Internally modeled on published government baselines. Official CBO/JCT scoring pending and welcomed.
Contact: contact@theamericandeal.org

These figures are preliminary model estimates built on published CBO, JCT, IRS, Treasury,
OECD, BEA, GAO, SSA, HHS-OIG, USDA-OIG, and peer-reviewed baselines. They are NOT an official
CBO or JCT score. Every file carries a methodology_version and last_updated column.

FILES
-----
nsra_revenue_streams.csv
  One row per scored revenue or cost stream (15 revenue + 2 program-linked costs).
  stream                          : plain-language name of the stream
  statutory_section               : NSRA section or title authorizing it
  year1_estimate_billion_usd      : Year-1 estimate, $B (negative = program-linked cost)
  baseline_source                 : published agency/source the estimate is built on
  base_year                       : year of the baseline data
  confidence                      : High / Moderate / Preliminary
  behavioral_sensitive            : Yes if elasticity-sensitive (subject to behavioral haircut)

nsra_fiscal_projections.csv
  10-year projection, central scenario.
  fiscal_year                          : 1-10
  net_revenue_billion_usd              : modeled net revenue that year
  program_outlays_billion_usd          : revenue-funded program outlays
  annual_deficit_reduction_billion_usd : net_revenue minus program_outlays
  Note: Year-1 realization ramps (86/94/98/100 pct across years 1-4); 2 pct/yr revenue growth assumed.

nsra_state_impact.csv
  One row per state (50 states). Population fields in millions (M) or thousands (K) as labeled.
  uninsured_M, snap_M, student_loans_M, veterans_M, renters_K, mfg_jobs_K, births_K, hospitals,
  median_income_K, elderly_pct, poverty_pct, uncompensated_care_B, insurer_mlr_pct.
  Sources: Census ACS, KFF, USDA FNS, VA, BLS, BEA (2024 vintage).

nsra_title_to_bill.csv
  Maps each of the 36 titles to its coordinated bill (1-5). Titles XVI & XVII are integrated.
  Source: the public five-coordinated-bill packaging on TheAmericanDeal.org Policy Vault.

nsra_title_to_committee.csv
  Maps each title to the primary congressional committees of its coordinated bill.
  Committee assignment is at the bill level (primary committees of the containing bill).

nsra_source_register.csv
  Headline macro figures and per-stream figures with their baseline source, source year,
  and confidence. Use this to trace any published number to its origin.

CANONICAL TAXONOMY
------------------
Revenue is the sum of 12 scored base revenue streams plus supplemental enforcement recapture.
Gross ~$784.6B/yr -> ~$749B net (central) -> ~$554B annual deficit reduction after ~$196B
revenue-funded outlays. Balanced budget Year 4 central (range 3-5).
