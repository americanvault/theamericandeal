The Sovereign Return Engine™

How much would the NSRA return to your household? The Sovereign Return Engine™ estimates your family's benefit across healthcare, drug costs, student debt, housing, and the Household Structural Relief Node — based on your state and income.

What it calculates

The calculator combines the NSRA's five household-facing provisions — the Basic Medical Provision, G7 drug pricing, zero-interest student debt, housing stabilization, and the Child Prosperity Trust — into a single household estimate tailored to your state and income.

Honest, auditable math

The Sovereign Return Engine™ is powered by The 7-Subledger Math Engine™. Every figure traces to a public source — CBO, JCT, IRS, BEA, and OECD baselines — documented in Schedule G of the bill text.
