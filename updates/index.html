What’s new in the American Deal

Changes to the bill, the numbers and the site, newest first. The full text always reflects the latest version.

November 7, 2026

Open-source re-score: $5.0 trillion in ten-year deficit reduction

Every tax provision was re-checked with the open-source Tax-Calculator model and IRS income statistics, and the Social Security changes against the Social Security actuaries' published estimates. Social Security tax now applies only to wages above $400,000. A new Section 3412 counts savings only once they are scored or audited.

November 7, 2026

One application for every federal benefit (Sec. 3907)

A single application screens a household for Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid, SNAP and health coverage. Shared checks catch duplicate and mistaken payments, with privacy limits and a person reviewing every case. Each program keeps its own rules, and States choose whether to join.

November 7, 2026

Fiscal Sustainability Plan and American Resource Fund

Seven measures save about $305 billion a year at full effect, including spending caps, Medicare drug negotiation, Social Security solvency for future retirees and crop-insurance reform. New oil, gas and offshore-wind royalties go into a permanent fund.

November 7, 2026

The whole bill, searchable on one page

All 40 titles and 261 sections can now be searched and linked section by section, and the homepage shows the complete legislative and fiscal architecture.

November 6, 2026

2026 midterm results: what the new Congress can pass in 2027

House, Senate and governor results, every flipped seat, and the six NSRA planks with support from both parties that the 120th Congress can move first.
